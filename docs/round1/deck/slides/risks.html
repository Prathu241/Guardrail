What could go wrong, and our plan for it

RiskMitigation
Live models judge differently from the offline stand-insRun it in week 1. The checks layer does not depend on the model. Each disagreement becomes a red-team case.
Checks overfit to attacks we wroteHeld-out attacks by outsiders and an external benchmark, reported separately.
Too many escalations annoy users17 legitimate controls gate every change; false positives are a headline metric.
Model latency or costChecks first; read-only tools skip review; routing measured on cost and latency.
API outage during the demoBy design it fails closed to a person; the demo also runs fully offline.

Known limit: checks are domain-specific by design. A new tool needs its own checks and attack cases.

The main risk is that the live model behaves differently from our offline reviewer stand-ins, which is why that comparison is the first milestone.
